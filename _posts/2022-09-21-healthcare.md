"Last Mile Now" explores how chatbot and text messaging technologies could improve access to healthcare for low-income individuals and communities. By leveraging these tools, the organization could connect individuals with healthcare resources and information in real-time, making it easier for people to access the care they need. Additionally, chatbots and text messaging could help reduce the complexity of the healthcare system, making it more user-friendly and accessible for those who may not have access to other forms of technology. Chatbots could also allow "Last Mile Now" to reach a wider audience, including those who may not have access to the internet or a computer.
