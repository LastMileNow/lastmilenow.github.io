We are thrilled to announce a significant evolution in our organization's mission: **Vision 3.0**.

While LastMileNow has always been dedicated to bridging the gap between technology and people, we are now focusing our efforts specifically on the **"Last Mile" problem in AI**.

The models exist, but the bridge to everyday utility for non-technical users and small businesses (SMBs) is often missing. LastMileNow teaches AI to small businesses and seniors.

### What is Vision 3.0?

Our new mission centers on education, access, and trust:

*   **AI Driving School:** AI literacy for SMBs and senior citizen AI companionship.
*   **Open source and public goods:** Local-first AI kits and standardized data connectors.

### Why now?

The "Last Mile" is the most expensive and difficult part of any technology deployment. In the AI era, this is where the real value is created—not just in the models themselves, but in how they are integrated into the daily lives of people who aren't technical experts.

We are committed to:
*   **Humanizing AI:** Removing the tech-speak and focusing on utility.
*   **Protecting Privacy:** Exploring local-first AI kits.
*   **Ensuring Equity:** Speaking up for "Data Dignity" for small business owners.

Join us as we embark on this new chapter. Check out our [Initiatives](/initiatives) to learn more about our specific projects.

The future of AI is human-centric. Let's build it together.
