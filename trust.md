### Security, Privacy, and Verification

In the "Last Mile," trust is the most important currency. We ensure that AI tools are safe, private, and transparent.

#### "LastMile Certified" Badge
We've created a verification seal for AI tools that meet specific safety, privacy, and simplicity standards. When you see the badge, you know the tool is "SMB-Safe."

#### Compliance-as-a-Service
Our automated tools scrub sensitive customer information (PII) from communications before they are sent to large language models. This ensures GDPR/CCPA compliance for small businesses without the need for expensive legal teams.

#### Local-First AI
We advocate for and develop tools that keep your data on your hardware. By prioritizing local inference, we eliminate the risk of data leaks to third-party providers.

> "AI shouldn't come at the cost of your privacy or your customers' trust."
