## What we do

We develop and maintain public goods that bridge the gap between advanced AI and practical utility. Our focus is on privacy, accessibility, and interoperability.

### Local-First AI Kits
We plan to develop and release open-source "starter kits" for running small, private LLMs locally on consumer hardware. This ensures that user privacy is protected at the "last mile" without requiring expensive cloud infrastructure.

### Standardized Data Connectors
We plan to build open-source connectors that allow mom-and-pop shops to safely plug their data (Excel sheets, PDFs, legacy CRMs) into AI systems without technical debt or security risks.

### Raising Awareness & Literacy
We advocate for the use of open-source models as a way to maintain "Data Dignity" and prevent vendor lock-in for small businesses.
