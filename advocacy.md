### Protecting the "Last Mile" Workforce

As AI transforms the economy, we advocate for the individuals and small businesses who are most affected by the transition.

#### Data Dignity for SMBs
We lobby for rights regarding how small business data is used to train larger models. Your business's expertise shouldn't be extracted without your consent or compensation.

#### AI Safety Nets
We conduct research on how AI impacts local labor markets. Our goal is to ensure the "last mile" workforce—the delivery drivers, the technicians, the front-office staff—is *augmented* by AI, not just replaced by it.

#### Standardized Data Connectors
We build and release open-source connectors that allow mom-and-pop shops to safely plug their messy data (Excel sheets, PDFs, handwritten notes) into LLMs without incurring massive technical debt.

#### The Guardian: Privacy and Security
We build tools specifically designed to prevent SMBs from accidentally leaking trade secrets or client data to public models. We believe that security should be a default, not a premium feature.

> "The "Last Mile" is about more than just technology—it's about the people and businesses that form the backbone of our economy. We are here to ensure they have a seat at the table."
