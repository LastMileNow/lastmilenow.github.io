### Security, Privacy, and Verification

In the "Last Mile," trust is the most important currency. Our aim is to help people use AI tools safely, privately, and with their eyes open.

#### Choosing AI Tools Wisely
We want to help non-technical users ask good questions about the AI tools they adopt: what happens to my data, who can see it, and how easy is it to leave?

#### Protecting Sensitive Information
We aim to teach practical habits, such as leaving customers' personal information out of prompts. Legal and regulatory requirements vary, so organizations should seek qualified advice about their own obligations.

#### Local-First AI
We are interested in tools that keep data on your own hardware. Running models locally can reduce how much information is shared with third-party providers.

> "AI shouldn't come at the cost of your privacy or your customers' trust."
