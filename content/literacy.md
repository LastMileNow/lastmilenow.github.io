### Education & AI Literacy

We believe that the power of AI should be accessible to everyone, not just those with technical degrees. Our "AI Driving School" initiative is focused on humanizing technology.

#### "AI Driving School" for SMBs
We offer free or low-cost workshops teaching small business owners (plumbers, accountants, florists) how to actually *use* generative AI for tangible tasks. 
*   **Customer Communication:** Writing polite but firm collection emails.
*   **Marketing:** Generating engaging social media captions and local ad copy.
*   **Operations:** Automating repetitive administrative tasks without complex code.

#### Senior Citizen AI Companionship
We develop programs focused on voice-first AI interfaces to help seniors:
*   Combat loneliness through conversational agents.
*   Manage medication and appointment schedules.
*   Simplify technology interaction by removing the need for complex screens.

#### The "Prompt Library" for Real Life
We maintain an open-source, curated library of high-utility prompts specifically for non-technical scenarios:
*   Negotiating medical bills.
*   Understanding complex lease agreements.
*   Drafting formal letters for community advocacy.

> "The goal isn't just to teach people how to prompt; it's to teach them how to thrive in an AI-augmented world."
