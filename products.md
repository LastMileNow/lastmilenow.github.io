### Vertical-Specific AI Solutions

The big AI companies focus on the "average" user. We focus on the *specific* user in industries that have been neglected by the tech revolution.

#### Niche "Micro-SaaS" Wrappers
We build highly specific, stripped-down AI interfaces designed for one task and one industry:
*   **EstimateGenerator:** For general contractors to turn site notes into professional bids.
*   **ShiftScheduler:** For independent restaurants to manage complex staff rotations seamlessly.
*   **ClientNote:** For solo therapists to safely summarize session notes while maintaining strict privacy.

#### The "Last Mile" Hardware
We are developing the **LMN-1 Dongle**, a physical plug-and-play device that sits on a local network. It acts as a:
*   **Secure AI Firewall:** Scrubbing sensitive data before it leaves the office.
*   **Local Inference Server:** Running private models for sensitive client data that should never touch the cloud.

#### Enterprise-Grade Support for the Little Guy
We provide SLA-backed APIs and "Compliance-as-a-Service," ensuring that even a solo practitioner has the same level of security and reliability as a Fortune 500 company.
